# Template 06 - AI Focus

## Purpose

This template gives the portfolio a future-facing AI-native identity. It explains how the developer fits into modern agentic and LLM-driven workflows.

## Branding Rationale

- Supports the brand shift from pure MERN identity to leverage-driven engineering.
- Signals interest in agentic systems, structured outputs, and automation.
- Helps recruiters see forward momentum instead of a static stack summary.

## Design Decisions

- Uses a workflow rail to make AI thinking feel operational rather than abstract.
- Pairs strategic pillars with a practical stack summary.
- Keeps the palette aligned with the rest of the portfolio while introducing a more futuristic narrative.

## Animation Philosophy

- Workflow steps should reveal sequentially.
- The section should feel intelligent and measured, not flashy.

## Reusability Strategy

- Suitable for AI services, automation consulting, or personal product thinking.
- Can be updated later with real model names, toolchains, or shipped AI features.

## Recommended Usage

- Place near the skills or experience section.
- Use it to support future-proof personal branding.
- Keep the language concrete and avoid hype without implementation.
