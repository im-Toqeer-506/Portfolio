# Template 04 - Projects

## Purpose

This template converts work history into premium product storytelling. It should help projects feel like launches, not list items.

## Branding Rationale

- Reinforces the portfolio as a serious product and engineering narrative.
- Elevates the strongest project to featured status.
- Creates a proof layer that speaks to recruiters, founders, and technical reviewers.

## Design Decisions

- Large featured panel on the left for the main story.
- Smaller supporting cards on the right to show range without distraction.
- Metric blocks and tags turn feature lists into scan-friendly evidence.

## Animation Philosophy

- The featured card should arrive first and carry the focal weight.
- Supporting cards should step in more softly.
- Action links should feel crisp and immediate.

## Reusability Strategy

- The featured project, supporting projects, and results are data-driven.
- The template can also be used for startup case studies, freelance work, or product launches.

## Recommended Usage

- Place after the skills section.
- Keep the number of projects small and highly curated.
- Favor proof, clarity, and results over quantity.
