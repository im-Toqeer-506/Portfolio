# Template 08 - Testimonials

## Purpose

This template adds social proof to the portfolio so the brand feels validated by collaborators, peers, or clients.

## Branding Rationale

- Testimonials make the presentation feel more trustworthy.
- Short quotes are easier to scan than long paragraphs.
- The section helps balance self-authored messaging with external validation.

## Design Decisions

- A side proof panel anchors the layout.
- Quote cards feel editorial and premium.
- The structure works well when only a small number of testimonials are available.

## Animation Philosophy

- Cards should ease into place without feeling like a carousel gimmick.
- Motion should support trust, not entertainment.

## Reusability Strategy

- The section can hold quotes, client notes, colleague feedback, or recommendation snippets.
- The proof metrics can be swapped for ratings, logos, or engagement stats.

## Recommended Usage

- Use only when the quotes are real and relevant.
- Keep the language concise and concrete.
- Place near the contact section to reduce conversion friction.
