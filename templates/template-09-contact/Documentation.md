# Template 09 - Contact

## Purpose

This template turns contact into a premium conversion block instead of a generic form footer.

## Branding Rationale

- Keeps the outreach experience polished and professional.
- Repeats the core identity through channels and CTA copy.
- Helps the portfolio end with action, not just decoration.

## Design Decisions

- Splits social proof and form entry into two clear panels.
- Uses a collaboration-first message instead of a transactional one.
- Keeps the form design calm and easy to scan.

## Animation Philosophy

- The section should feel welcoming and focused.
- Motion should guide attention to the form and the channel list.

## Reusability Strategy

- Works for hiring, freelance, partnership, or project inquiry flows.
- All channel data and copy are easily replaceable.

## Recommended Usage

- Use as the final major content section before the footer.
- Keep the CTA language direct and low-friction.
