# Template 07 - Experience

## Purpose

This template shows growth over time. It is designed to make the developer look intentional, evolving, and capable of handling increasingly complex work.

## Branding Rationale

- A timeline makes progress easier to trust than a static bio block.
- Helps position the developer as someone with momentum and direction.
- Connects academic background to real product execution.

## Design Decisions

- Uses a split layout to balance proof and narrative.
- The left panel gives context and measurable structure.
- The right timeline keeps the growth story visually engaging.

## Animation Philosophy

- Timeline cards should reveal in sequence.
- The section should feel composed and professional.
- Motion should support reading order, not compete with it.

## Reusability Strategy

- The timeline can represent jobs, internships, milestone projects, or community growth.
- The stats and impacts can be replaced by measurable outcomes.

## Recommended Usage

- Place after projects or open source.
- Use it to show maturity, consistency, and long-term direction.
